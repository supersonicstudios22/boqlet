# 03 — Casual one-time user review

Reviewer persona: a small contractor / freelance estimator with one project and a few days of work. Needs to
upload an IFC file, get a sensible BoQ, adjust a few things and export to Excel or PDF. Not a BIM expert;
dislikes jargon.

Session: 2026-11-23, 09:01–09:34, local stack (`http://localhost:3000`), `main` at `3868013`. Account
`casual@review.test` (new, Free plan), org "Casey's Renovations" (`/casey-s-renovations`), project "Duplex
on Elm Street". Driven through a real Chromium (Playwright, 1440×900, one context) via
`docs/review/_scratch/cas/driver.mjs`; screenshots in `docs/review/_screenshots/cas/`; downloaded workbooks
in `docs/review/_scratch/cas/x1/`.

---

## 1. Verdict

I got from an invitation link to a first, readable BoQ of the Duplex in about ten minutes, and the parts I
care about are good: fast processing, plain line descriptions, a "How it is calculated" panel I can trust,
edits that require a reason and show up in the Excel, and a simple share link. But I would **not finish a
real project with it yet and would not pay today**. My second file (the IFC4 sample house) came back with
a ground floor of **146,199 m²** and 13,243 m³ of concrete, and the app's "recommended" answer kept the
wrong number. If I had not spotted it, I would have priced a house a thousand times too big. I also sent an
Excel to "my client" that silently lacked my edits, there is no PDF, and the Excel prints across six pages
with quantities on a different page from descriptions. Add the forced 15-minute setup full of
"classifications", "dictionaries" and "slots", blocking questions that ask what "Damp-proofing" is with no
matching answer, and plans that all say "Price on request", and a one-off user like me would most likely
give up and go back to measuring by hand.

## 5. Assumptions and judgement calls

- I treated the `npm run local:user` link as the invitation email a real user would get; the account is on
  the default Free plan.
- I typed what a casual user would type: "euro" and "Ireland" in onboarding (to test the fields), and the
  default "As modelled" start in the wizard. I used "Use the suggested answers" and both "Skip" buttons,
  because a one-off user would.
- I did not tick any classification in the first "Generate a BoQ" dialog because every option was labelled
  "ACME" or "Demo" or was a UK system; later I added "Demo: trade sections" to see what grouping looks like.
- For the S05 questions I accepted the recommended thickness answer and deliberately chose the
  non-recommended "measured from the geometry" volume answer to see an effect; "Leave it" for the two
  space-name questions. For S09 I picked the closest search terms I could find (Aluminium, Steel, Air gap,
  Membrane for damp-proofing, Finish for laminate) and the geometry volume for both slabs.
- The S09 file's own GrossVolume is wrong (a Revit 2015 exporter mm-scale bug); I still rate CAS-001
  critical because the app's recommended answer and its area derivation turn a file error into a ×1000
  BoQ error while a correct GrossArea is present in the same file.
- "Save as PDF from Excel" was simulated by exporting the BoQ sheet to PDF through Excel COM automation on
  this machine (`_scratch/cas/x1/boq-print.pdf`); I could not render the PDF to an image, so the
  page-break evidence is Excel's own page-break report.
- I did not click "Choose" on a paid plan: the click was blocked by the session's permission guard; the
  CAS-012 consequence is taken from the app's message catalogue (medium confidence).
- I did not wait for a big parse; S10 was rejected before upload by the Free limit, as expected.
- The Next.js dev indicator ("N" button, "Compiling…") and first-compile delays are dev-server artefacts
  and not counted as findings. Load times in the log include them.
- Times are from a scripted browser; a real person reading the screens would be slower. I saw no queueing
  behind other reviewers' jobs except the 33 s and 47 s regenerations.
- I did not judge measuring rules in depth (that is the QS reviewer's job); CAS-017 is flagged at medium
  confidence from the arithmetic and the type name only.
- One 503 response appeared in the browser console during the session; I could not tie it to a step, so it
  is not a finding.
- Account data left behind: org "Casey's Renovations" with one project (3 models, 2 BoQs, saved version A,
  one active share link expiring 2026-12-23). Driver script and helpers are in `docs/review/_scratch/cas/`.
